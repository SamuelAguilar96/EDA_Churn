A bank is experiencing a high customer churn rate and has requested an in-depth analysis to understand which types
of customers are leaving the bank and why.

I performed an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to identify the key factors using Python (Pandas, matplotlib,
seaborn, Scikit-learn, SHAP).

I discovered that churn was driven by two main issues: low customer engagement and dissatisfaction among highly
engaged customers. Overall, low engagement was the primary driver of churn, but dissatisfaction led to churn among
"high-value" customers. Features such as nationality, account balance, age, and activity were significant determinants.
Additionally, developing new features (feature engineering) played a crucial role in predicting churn.



Un banco está experimentando una alta tasa de abandono de clientes (churn) y solicita una análisis profundo para
conocer con certeza qué tipo de cliente abandona la entidad y el por qué

Realicé un Análisis Exploratorio de Datos para identificar estos factores clave utilizando Python (Pandas,
matplotlib, seaborn, Scikit-learn, SHAP.

Descubrí que el abandono se debía a dos problemas: la baja vinculación de los clientes y la insatisfacción de
los clientes con alta vinculación. En números totales, la baja vinculación determinaba mayor churn, pero la insatisfacción
provocaba el churn de clientes de "alto valor". Características como la nacionalidad, saldo en caja, edad y actividad
son determinantes. El desarrollo de nuevas características (feature engineering) también ayudó a predecir el churn.
